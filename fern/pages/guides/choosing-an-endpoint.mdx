title: Choosing the Right Call
description: Which endpoint answers which question, and the patterns that keep calls fast and cheap.

KINETK has three kinds of call. Picking the right one is most of what makes an integration fast and cheap.

| Kind of call | Endpoints | Speed | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Precomputed reads** — the narrative and pattern maps, rebuilt on a schedule | `GET /narratives/*`, `GET /intelligence/patterns/*` | under 2 s | free |
| **Synchronous search** — one bounded, live retrieval | `POST /search` | a few seconds | $0.010 per row returned |
| **Async jobs** — deep retrieval and analysis over the live corpus | `POST /intelligence/jobs` (`records`, `insights`, `patterns`, `stats`) | 10 s to a few minutes | per record or flat |

## Which call answers my question?

| You want to… | Call | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Show what's trending right now | `GET /narratives/trending` | Precomputed, free, instant. |
| Find narratives about a topic that already exist | `GET /narratives/search?q=…` | Searches the precomputed map by name and tags. |
| Drill into one narrative: its posts, creators, platform split | `GET /narratives/{id}` | Ids come from trending, search, `POST /search` and `insights`. |
| See whether a narrative is accelerating or fading | `GET /narratives/{id}/trajectory` | Its metric history across rebuilds. |
| Give an agent fresh content + context for a query, many times per task | `POST /search` | One call returns ranked posts *and* the narratives, patterns and creators they belong to. |
| Pull hundreds or thousands of matching posts | `records` job | Up to 10,000 records per job; large results come back as a download link. |
| Get the analytical "so what" about a topic | `insights` job | Narratives with trajectory and sentiment, tag and theme signals, platform opportunities. |
| Understand what content *looks like* and how visual themes connect | `GET /intelligence/patterns/*` or a `patterns` job | The pattern map clusters posts by image/video similarity, not tags. |
| Size a topic: how many posts, how much engagement, which platforms | `stats` job | An exact count over everything matching, not a sample. |

## Recipes

### An agent researching a topic

1. `POST /search` with the user's question. Read `content` for evidence and `narratives` for the stories it belongs to.
2. For a narrative worth explaining, `GET /narratives/{id}` for its full evidence, and `GET /narratives/{id}/trajectory` for its momentum.
3. If the agent needs the full analysis, submit an `insights` job and poll it while the agent keeps working.

Each step hands the next an id, so the agent walks the graph instead of re-searching.

### A dashboard

Poll `GET /narratives/trending` per window for the headline cards, and link each card to `GET /narratives/{id}`. These reads are free and sub-second, so refresh as often as your UI needs. Use `GET /health` to show data freshness.

### A weekly research report

Submit one `insights` job per topic with `includeSignals: true` for the written takeaways, plus a `stats` job for the exact size of each topic. Both run async — submit them all, then poll.

### Visual and creative research

Start with `GET /intelligence/patterns` (browse) or a `patterns` job (match a question to the map). Follow a pattern's `lookalikes` to find topics that look alike but are tagged differently — connections keyword search will not surface.

## Making calls fast and cheap

- **Start with the free reads.** If a precomputed narrative or pattern answers the question, you don't need a live retrieval.
- **Size `limit` to what you will read.** On `POST /search` and `records` you pay per row returned, and smaller limits come back faster.
- **Pick the narrowest `window` that answers the question.** It doesn't change the price, but `7d` and `30d` retrieve fresher, more focused evidence than `all`.
- **Keep `POST /search` for interactive loops.** If you need more than 300 rows or a full analysis, submit a job instead of paging through search.
- **Expect a `202` from `POST /search` now and then.** When a search runs past its time budget it hands the question to an `insights` job and returns a `jobId` — poll it like any other job.
- **Send an `Idempotency-Key` when you retry a search,** so a network retry never pays twice.
- **Don't resubmit a running job.** An identical submit while the first is running returns the same `jobId` (`dedup: true`); just poll.
- **Poll jobs every 2–5 seconds,** and allow up to a few minutes for `window: "all"` and large pulls.
- **Leave `includeGraphs` off on `insights`** unless you are drawing the graph — it makes the payload much larger.
- **Use `returnRecords` on `insights`** only when you need the evidence posts inline; narratives already carry their representative posts.
