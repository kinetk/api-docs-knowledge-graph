title: Choosing the Right Call
description: Which endpoint answers which question, and the patterns that keep calls fast and cheap.

KINETK has three kinds of call. Picking the right one is most of what makes an integration fast and cheap.

| Kind of call | Endpoints | Speed | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Precomputed reads** — the narrative and pattern maps, rebuilt on a schedule | `GET /narratives/*`, `GET /intelligence/patterns/*` | under 2 s | free |
| **Fast search** — real-time retrieval and analysis (max 300 records) | `POST /search` | a few seconds | 0.01 credits per record |
| **Async jobs** — deep retrieval and analysis over the live corpus | `POST /intelligence/jobs` (`records`, `insights`, `patterns`, `stats`) | 10 s to a few minutes | per record or flat |

<Note>
**Most calls take a time `window`: `7d`, `30d`, `90d` or `all`.** It sets how far back to look, from the last week to everything in the corpus. The window never changes the price.
</Note>

## Which call answers my question?

### Ask about a topic

Start with search. Each of the others goes further in one direction.

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Get a quick read on a topic" icon="magnifying-glass" href="/documentation/guides/search">
    **`POST /search`** · 0.01 credits per record

    See which narratives and posts are out there, in seconds. Returns ranked posts plus the narratives, patterns and creators they belong to. The best first call for almost any question.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Go deeper on what search found" icon="chart-line" href="/documentation/guides/intelligence-jobs">
    **`insights` job** · 0.015 credits per record analyzed

    Learn what's rising, how people feel about it and where the opportunities are. Analyzes up to 10,000 posts for narrative trajectory, sentiment, tag and theme signals, and platform opportunities.
  </Card>
  <Card title="See what content looks like" icon="images" href="/documentation/guides/pattern-intelligence">
    **`patterns` job** · 10 credits per call

    Find the visual themes behind a topic. Matches your question to the pattern map, which groups posts by image and video similarity rather than tags, and returns the closest patterns with a written summary.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Measure how big a topic is" icon="chart-simple" href="/documentation/guides/intelligence-jobs">
    **`stats` job** · 5 credits per call

    See how much of the corpus is about your query. Exact totals over everything that matches, not a sample: posts, views, likes, comments and engagement, per platform.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Get just the social posts" icon="list" href="/documentation/guides/intelligence-jobs">
    **`records` job** · 0.01 credits per record

    Pull the matching posts themselves, in bulk: up to 10,000 ranked posts per job. Large results come back as a download link.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
Not sure where to start? Run `POST /search` with your question. If the answer is interesting, send the same query to an `insights` job to go deeper.
</Tip>

### Explore narratives (free)

These read the precomputed maps. They're instant and cost nothing, so use them freely.

| You want to… | Call |
|---|---|
| See what's trending right now | `GET /narratives/trending` |
| Find existing narratives about a topic | `GET /narratives/search?q=…` |
| Dig into one narrative: its posts, creators and platform split | `GET /narratives/{id}` |
| Check if a narrative is growing or fading | `GET /narratives/{id}/trajectory` |

Narrative ids come from search, trending and `insights`, so you can go straight from a search result to `GET /narratives/{id}` and its trajectory without searching again. These reads are free and instant, so they're a good base for dashboards you refresh often.

## Keeping costs down

- **Start with the free reads.** If a narrative or pattern already answers the question, you don't need a paid call.
- **Size `limit` to what you'll read.** You pay per record, and smaller limits come back faster.
- **Pick the narrowest `window`.** It doesn't change the price, but `7d` and `30d` return fresher, more focused results than `all`.
- **Use a job for anything over 300 records.** Search tops out at 300; a `records` or `insights` job goes up to 10,000.
