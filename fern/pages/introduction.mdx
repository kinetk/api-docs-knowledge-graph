The **KINETK Graph Service API** is the public surface of the KINETK Knowledge Graph — a multimodal content intelligence platform that retrieves, clusters and explains social-media content, narratives and visual patterns across TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, Pinterest, X, Snapchat and more.

<Card title="Get Your API Key" icon="key" href="https://platform.kinetk.ai">
  Create a key in the KINETK platform to start making requests.
</Card>

There are two ways to use it:

- **HTTP API** — REST endpoints behind an API key. Best for backends, dashboards, scheduled jobs and agents you build yourself.
- **MCP server** — a hosted [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server that exposes the graph as tools in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex and other AI assistants.

Both reach the same backend and bill the same account.

## What you can do

| Capability | Endpoint | Speed | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ranked content + its narratives and creators, in one call | `POST /search` | a few seconds | $0.010 / row |
| Trending narratives | `GET /narratives/trending` | under 2 s | free |
| Search the narrative map | `GET /narratives/search` | under 2 s | free |
| One narrative: posts, creators, platform split | `GET /narratives/{id}` | under 3 s | free |
| A narrative's momentum over time | `GET /narratives/{id}/trajectory` | under 2 s | free |
| Browse the visual pattern map | `GET /intelligence/patterns` | under 2 s | free |
| One pattern, its posts and its lookalikes | `GET /intelligence/patterns/{patternKey}` (+ `/content`, `/lookalikes`) | under 2 s | free |
| Deep retrieval and analysis: `records`, `insights`, `patterns`, `stats` | `POST /intelligence/jobs` + `GET /intelligence/jobs/{id}` | async | per record or flat |
| Your credit history | `GET /usage` | under 1 s | free |
| Service status and data freshness | `GET /health` | under 1 s | free |

Three kinds of call sit behind that table:

- **Precomputed reads** of the narrative and pattern maps, rebuilt on a schedule — instant and free.
- **Synchronous search** — one bounded live retrieval, for agents and apps that call many times per task.
- **Async jobs** — deep retrieval and analysis over the live corpus. Submit returns a `jobId` immediately; poll until it succeeds. Identical requests are served from cache.

Not sure which to use? See [Choosing the Right Call](./guides/choosing-an-endpoint.mdx).

## Core concepts

- **Content** — a post or video, with normalized relevance, engagement and recency signals and the score it is ranked by. See [The Content Object](./guides/content-object.mdx).
- **Narratives** — clusters of content a community is talking about, tracked over time with momentum, lifecycle and sentiment. See [Narrative Intelligence](./guides/narrative-intelligence.mdx).
- **Patterns** — groups of content that *look* alike, discovered from images and video, with the visual elements they share. See [Pattern Intelligence](./guides/pattern-intelligence.mdx).

## Base URL

```
https://api.kinetk.ai/graph
```

Every endpoint requires your secret key in the `x-api-key` header. Export these so the examples in these docs work as written:

```bash
export API_BASE="https://api.kinetk.ai/graph"
export API_KEY="<your key>"
```
